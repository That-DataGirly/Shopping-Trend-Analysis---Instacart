## 1. Executive Summary  

This project was conducted to improve transaction reliability, understand customer purchasing behavior, evaluate retail sales performance, and forecast product demand using Instacart transaction data.  

The analysis identified data quality issues, uncovered key revenue drivers, analyzed customer purchasing patterns, and developed a predictive model to support inventory planning and operational decision-making.  

---

## 2. Business Problem  

Instacart's leadership reported limited confidence in existing sales reports due to inconsistent transaction records, missing information, and limited visibility into product performance.  

The company needed answers to several business questions:  

- Can transaction data be trusted?  
- Which products drive revenue?  
- Which locations perform best?  
- How do customers purchase products?  
- How much inventory will be required in the future?
